How it works

From an account to your own broker.

AuraFX shows the scan. You decide whether to trade, and you place the order. We do not execute orders or hold funds.

  1. 1Create your account. Name, email, phone and country. Accept the risk warning and the terms.
  2. 2Use the web dashboard. Live quotes, the signal board, calendar, checklist and journal. The prices are public feeds, not your broker account.
  3. 3Install MT5 tools only if you want them. Optional indicators and EAs. MT4 linking is not the live path.
  4. 4Connect your broker. Download MetaTrader from a broker you choose. Start on demo if you are new. AuraFX never sees that password or a balance API.
  5. 5You place every trade. The board can say buy, sell or wait. You click in your own platform. Auto trading on AuraFX is off.
