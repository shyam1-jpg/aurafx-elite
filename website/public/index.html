FAQ

Short answers. The legal pages carry the full warnings.

Read the risk warning
Does AuraFX place the trade?

No. The default mode is analysis only. Auto trading is off and broker execution is not connected.

What does the confidence number mean?

It is setup quality until at least eight closed signals exist. After that, a card can show a measured rate from that history and it says so. The rate is not a forecast.

Why do some markets stay on Wait?

Wait is published when the rules for that regime are not met, news is inside the pause window, or volatility is too wide. The card names the reason.

Can I pay for Pro or Elite here?

No. The prices are listed in GBP, and payment is not taken on this site yet. Use Contact us to reach support@aurafxelite.com.
