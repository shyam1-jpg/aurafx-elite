AI engine

The engine calculates. AI explains.

Direction, levels and the regime come from rules on hourly and daily candles. A language model, when a key is configured, may rewrite the wording. It does not pick the side or move the stop.

1. Read the candlesTwelve Data when a key is set, then Yahoo Finance, then Kraken where a pair exists. These are not your broker’s quotes, and spread is not included.
2. Name the regimeTrending when ADX is at least 25. Ranging otherwise, unless the current bar or ATR is more than 2.2 times its recent norm, which is volatile. News is a separate pause, not a fourth regime.
3. Apply the rules for that regimeTrending markets publish a buy or sell only when trend, momentum, structure and the 4-hour plus daily trend all agree. Ranging mean-reversion is limited to the instruments in the range list, gold by default. Every other ranging market keeps the trend rules. Volatile markets publish nothing.
4. Refuse a weak setupVolatility has to be tradable, high-impact news has to sit outside the pause window, raw confidence has to clear the publication line, and risk/reward has to be at least 1.8. Otherwise the card is Wait.
5. Label the number honestlyConfidence starts as setup quality. After eight closed take-profit or stop results, the displayed number can switch to a measured rate from that history. The card says which one it is. The rate is not a forecast, and it does not change what gets published.

What “AI” changes

With no model key, the rule engine writes the note and answers the assistant from the current board. With a key, the model may rephrase that note. Both paths are blocked from promising profit or a fixed accuracy figure.

What this page does not do

The engine does not send orders. Auto trading is off. Broker execution is not connected. MT4 and MT5 tools, where offered, are optional chart software. You still click buy or sell at your broker.
