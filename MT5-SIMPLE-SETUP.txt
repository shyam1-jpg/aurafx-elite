WHY IT LOOKS LIKE NOTHING IS HAPPENING
--------------------------------------
1. INDICATORS do NOT trade — they only show signals/colors on the chart.
   (Chartist Pro, Elite Signals, Pro Assistant, Risk Guardian = visual only)

2. Only EXPERT ADVISORS (EAs) place trades — you need Algo Trading ON (green).

3. You had TOO MANY EAs on different charts at once — they fight each other.
   Each EA only manages ITS OWN trades (magic number).

4. Manual trades (you clicked Buy/Sell) are NOT trailed by the EA.
   Your log showed a manual XAUUSD buy with NO stop-loss — EA cannot trail that.

5. AuraFX Pro EA had NO trailing before — now fixed (re-install to update).


RECOMMENDED SETUP — ONE CHART TO START
--------------------------------------
Chart:  XAUUSD  H1  (gold, 1 hour)
EA:     AuraFX_Universal_AI_EA
         - Mode: Aggressive
         - InpUseTrailing = true
         - InpTrailStartAtr = 0.5
Indicator: AuraFX_Chartist_Pro (colors + patterns + SL/TP lines)

Steps:
  1. Close extra charts or remove other EAs from them
  2. Open XAUUSD H1
  3. Drag AuraFX_Chartist_Pro onto chart → OK
  4. Drag AuraFX_Universal_AI_EA onto SAME chart → OK → Allow Algo Trading
  5. Top toolbar: Algo Trading button must be GREEN
  6. Chart top-right: smiley face must be smiling (not X)

TRAILING STOP — WHEN IT WORKS
-----------------------------
- Trade must be opened BY THE EA (comment says "AuraFX Universal AI" etc.)
- InpUseTrailing must be true in EA settings
- Price must be in profit first:
    Universal AI: profit >= 0.5 x ATR (panel shows "Trail: ACTIVE")
    Pro EA: profit >= 120 points (default)
- You will see SL line move on the chart when trail is active
